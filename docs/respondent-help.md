# Help for respondents

You have been asked to take part in a survey about how people work together. This page explains how it works. The person who sent you the link can answer any other question.

## What the survey asks

The survey lists your colleagues and asks a few questions about your working relationship with each of them, for example how often you collaborate. There are no right or wrong answers. The introduction on the first screen says why the survey is being run and how the results will be used. Taking part is your choice: if you would rather not, close the page, and nothing is sent.

## Opening your link

Your link is personal. Open it on your phone, tablet or computer in a recent browser: Safari 16.4 or later (iOS 16.4 or later on iPhone and iPad), Chrome or Edge 80 or later, or Firefox 113 or later. The first screens ask you to agree to take part and to confirm your name. If the link names someone else, do not answer: tell the person who sent it.

Some surveys come with a survey file (its name ends in `.graticule-survey`). Save the file, then choose it when your link asks for it. On an iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the attachment in Mail and choose Save to Files; on Android, download the attachment; on a computer, save it.

## Answering

If the survey asks you to select colleagues first, select everyone you have a working relationship with. The screen says what is recorded for people you do not select. Then answer every question about each colleague you are asked about. Where a question has no answer for someone, for example the quality of a relationship you do not have, choose "Does not apply" if it is offered. You can check and change every answer before you finish.

Your answers are saved on this device as you go, so you can close the page and return to your link later. "Clear my answers from this device" deletes them at any time.

## Who will see your answers

Your answers are confidential, and they carry your name. The people running the survey will see which ratings you gave, because mapping how people work together needs to know who each rating came from. The confidentiality statement on the first screen says who will see your answers and the results. The survey does not assess anyone, and the results should not be used to evaluate individual performance.

## Sending your answers back

When you finish, your answers are encrypted on your device: only the people running the survey can open them. Nothing is sent automatically. Either copy the encrypted text and paste it into an email, or download it as a file and attach it, as the instructions on the screen say. Your answers are then cleared from the device.

Keep the receipt code shown at the end. Quote it if you contact the survey team about your response. If you send more than one response, the latest one counts.
